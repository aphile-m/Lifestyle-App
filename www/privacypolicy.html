Trainer App — Health Data Privacy

Trainer App is a personal fitness app. It reads the following from Health Connect, with your explicit permission: steps, sleep sessions, and resting heart rate.

How the data is used

The data feeds your personal Lifestyle Score and your AI coach's context. It is stored on your device and synced only to your own private Supabase database account, protected by your login. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Your control

You can revoke access at any time in the Health Connect app, and delete synced data by deleting rows from your own database. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data.
